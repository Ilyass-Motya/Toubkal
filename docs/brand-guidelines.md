# Toubkal Browser Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-18

This document establishes the visual and verbal identity guidelines for Toubkal Browser, ensuring consistency across all touchpoints and maintaining our privacy-first, mountain-inspired brand identity.

---

## Brand Identity Overview

Toubkal Browser is the intelligent browser that protects your mind. Our brand identity reflects:

- **Privacy-First**: Every design decision prioritizes user privacy and data protection
- **Mountain-Inspired**: Drawing from Mount Toubkal's strength, stability, and natural beauty
- **Intelligent**: AI-powered features that enhance user experience without compromising privacy
- **Transparent**: Open, honest communication about data handling and browser operations

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
The Toubkal Browser logo consists of:
- Mountain icon (🏔️) in Toubkal Blue
- "Toubkal Browser" text in Deep Mountain
- Tagline: "The intelligent browser that protects your mind"

### Logo Variations

#### Horizontal
- Full logo with text and tagline
- Minimum width: 200px
- Use in headers, footers, marketing materials

#### Vertical
- Stacked logo with icon above text
- Minimum height: 100px
- Use in mobile interfaces, square spaces

#### Icon Only
- Mountain icon only
- Minimum size: 32px
- Use in favicons, app icons, small spaces

### Logo Spacing
- **Clear space:** Equal to the height of the mountain icon
- **Minimum size:** 32px for icon, 200px for horizontal logo
- **Maximum size:** No limit, maintain proportions

### Logo Usage Rules
- ✅ Always maintain clear space around the logo
- ✅ Use approved color variations only
- ✅ Ensure sufficient contrast against backgrounds
- ❌ Don't distort or alter the logo proportions
- ❌ Don't place logo on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Don't use unapproved color combinations

## Tone of Voice

### Brand Personality

#### Intelligent
- Use clear, technical language when appropriate
- Explain complex concepts in simple terms
- Provide helpful context and explanations

#### Privacy-Focused
- Be transparent about data handling
- Use reassuring language about security
- Emphasize local processing and control

#### Mountain-Inspired
- Use strong, confident language
- Emphasize stability and reliability
- Avoid overly casual or trendy expressions

### Writing Guidelines

#### Voice Characteristics
- **Clear and Direct:** Get to the point quickly
- **Reassuring:** Build confidence in privacy features
- **Helpful:** Provide guidance and support
- **Honest:** Be transparent about limitations

#### Tone Examples

**Good:**
- "Your data stays on your device"
- "AI processing happens locally"
- "We don't collect personal information"
- "Choose your privacy level"

**Avoid:**
- "We're super excited about..."
- "This is amazing!"
- "You'll love this feature"
- "Trust us with your data"

### Content Guidelines

#### Headlines
- Use action-oriented language
- Focus on benefits, not features
- Keep under 60 characters when possible

#### Body Text
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Include bullet points for lists
- Use active voice
- Avoid jargon and technical terms

#### Error Messages
- Be specific about what went wrong
- Provide clear next steps
- Use helpful, not accusatory language
- Include toubkal:// URL examples

## Accessibility Guidelines

### Color Contrast
- **Normal text:** Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- **Large text:** Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio
- **UI components:** Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio
- **Focus indicators:** Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio

### Typography
- **Minimum font size:** 12px for body text
- **Line height:** Minimum 1.4 for body text
- **Letter spacing:** Normal (0) for body text
- **Word spacing:** Normal (0.25em) for body text

### Interactive Elements
- **Focus indicators:** Visible on all interactive elements
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44px for mobile
- **Keyboard navigation:** All interactive elements accessible
- **Screen readers:** Proper ARIA labels and roles
